Welcome to Our Church

Victory in Jesus Evangelistic Center Church

“We Win” 1 Corinthians 15:57-58

Victory in Jesus Evangelistic Center Church is the place to be! We are an exciting, Christ-centered, anointed worship, Non-Denominational Bible Church. Part of the vision and burden of our church is to assist in the transformation process of an ordinary individual into a powerful, effective, and mature Believer of Jesus Christ!

At Victory, we envision creatively implementing the Great Commission and building a growing community of mature believers, united in purpose and equipped for effective ministry (Matthew 28:19-20).

Triumph Christian Academy

Helping Children Grow and Flourish

As a parent, we know that you want to provide your child with the best education – and that is something that we want to help you with at Triumph Christian Academy. We strive to provide your child with an opportunity to grow and flourish in a holistic way through our early childhood development programs.

Global Discipleship Ministry

Partnership Through Compassion International

Victory in Jesus has partnered with Compassion International to create the Global Discipleship Ministry.

Our purpose is to release children from global poverty holistically through the local church in communities of African descent one child, one family, one community at a time.
